Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

LinDesk is developed by junlangliuqiao. It runs a Debian-based Linux environment on your Android device.

1. Local data

Your Linux files and settings are stored in LinDesk’s private app storage. Runtime logs are handled locally. LinDesk does not automatically upload this data, clipboard text or microphone audio to us, and includes no ads or usage analytics.

2. Files, clipboard and microphone

LinDesk accesses files you select for import or export and shares clipboard text between Android and Linux for copy and paste. When you grant permission and enable microphone access, Linux apps can record audio. You can manage permissions in Android settings.

3. Third-party services

Software downloads, Linux apps, websites and AI tools such as Codex may send information to the services you use, including connection details and content you provide. Those services handle data under their own privacy policies.

4. Data retention and deletion

Your files remain on your device until you delete them. You can manage LinDesk’s stored data in Android settings. If you email us, we use your address and message for support, keep them only as long as needed for that purpose, and accept deletion requests by email.

5. Contact and updates

For privacy questions or deletion requests, email junlangliuqiao@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy will appear on this page with an updated date.
